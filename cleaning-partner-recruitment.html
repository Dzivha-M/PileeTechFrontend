Join Our Growing Network

Pilee Cleaning is recruiting independent cleaning partners to join our growing cleaning services network. We are looking for individuals with experience in:

  • Residential and domestic cleaning
  • Deep house cleaning
  • Office cleaning
  • Apartment cleaning
  • Airbnb cleaning
  • Laundry and ironing
  • Window cleaning
  • Outdoor cleaning
  • Move-in and move-out cleaning

How Does It Work?

The process from registration to payment.

  1. Register as an independent cleaning partner.
  2. Choose the cleaning services you specialise in.
  3. Indicate your availability and preferred working areas.
  4. Receive cleaning job opportunities through Pilee Cleaning.
  5. Accept suitable assignments.
  6. Complete jobs and receive weekly payments.
